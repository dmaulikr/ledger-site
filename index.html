Money has two shapes.

There's the river — the monthly stream of what life costs. And there are the pools — the projects you pour into over months or years. A home build. A wedding. A renovation. A trip.

Most money apps only show the river. Ledger shows both, kept separate — so "what has the house cost so far, and how much of that was cement?" is one tap away.

Log it in seconds

  • A fast keypad — amount, category, done.

Calm by design

Spending is never shown in alarm red. Ledger reflects; it never scolds. Budgets show what's left, never a warning. No streaks, ever.
