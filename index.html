Questions about Ledger

What is Ledger?

Ledger is a free, private money tracker for iPhone. It keeps your monthly spending and your long projects apart: each project, like building a house, gets its own pool with a running total, phases, a plan per category and PDF reports. Nothing leaves your phone, and there's no account.

Can I track house construction costs with it?

Yes. A project pool tracks sub-categories, phases, refunds that reduce the cost, a spending pace, and your plan against actual spending per category. You can add a cover photo and export a PDF report.

Can it log expenses automatically?

It can read bank SMS messages through a Shortcuts automation and read totals from photos of bills. A review inbox flags likely duplicates. You can also log by keypad, by typing or by voice.

Does it support rupees and UPI?

Yes. Indian Rupee is the default, with lakh and crore formatting. It tracks cash, UPI, card and bank separately, and takes foreign-currency entries at a rate you set.

Can it track loans and EMIs?

Yes. Record borrowing and lending with EMI schedules, flat or reducing interest, and due-date reminders. You can also split expenses by person or family.

Is my data private?

Your data stays on your iPhone. There's no account, the app can lock with Face ID, and the developer states that it collects no data.

Is Ledger free?

Yes. Ledger is free to download, with no in-app purchases.

Which devices does it work on?

iPhone with iOS 17 or later.
